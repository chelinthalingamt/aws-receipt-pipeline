Receipt Digitizer
Serverless on AWS · Sydney

Drop a receipt. Get the vendor, total and date in seconds.

Upload a PDF or a photo. It goes straight to Amazon S3, a Lambda function reads it, and the result is saved to DynamoDB and emailed out. Nothing runs until you upload.
